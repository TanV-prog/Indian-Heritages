Archival Guidelines

  • Authentic Community Voice: Prioritize traditions practiced by local elders and families.
  • Preserve Organic Knowledge: Include details on tools, natural plant dyes, seed varieties, or song meters.
  • Respect Customary Rights: Do not submit sacred rituals that communities ask to keep confidential.
  • Status Review: Administrators review submissions under /admin/ before approving for public display.
